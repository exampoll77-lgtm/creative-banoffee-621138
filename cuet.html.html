Syllabus

CUET syllabus: three sections, up to five papers

Every paper has 50 multiple-choice questions and runs for 60 minutes. A candidate can choose up to 5 papers.

Section I

Language

13 languages including English and Hindi.

  • Reading comprehension
  • Vocabulary and grammar
  • Literary aptitude
Section II

Domain subjects

23 subjects across Commerce, Humanities and Science.

  • Based on the NCERT Class 12 syllabus
  • Pick subjects your course asks for
  • Chapter-wise practice on Exampoll
Section III

General Aptitude Test

1 paper for courses that need it.

  • General knowledge and current affairs
  • Numerical ability and reasoning
  • Logical and analytical thinking

Get the official subject-wise syllabus from the NTA CUET website. Rules can change in the 2027 bulletin.

The 3-pass PYQ method
  • Pass 1: read each question without a timer
  • Pass 2: solve a full paper in 60 minutes
  • Pass 3: write down every mistake and its chapter
Previous year questions

Use old papers to learn the exam, not to guess it

Previous year questions show how the paper is framed and which chapters carry weight. Check the official NTA website for papers it has published, then practise the same style under a timer.

  • Start with the newest year
  • Review wrong answers, since each one costs 1 mark
  • Repeat with subject-wise mock tests

CUET 2027 eligibility

  • You should have passed Class 12 from a recognised board, or be appearing in 2027.
  • NTA does not set an age limit for CUET UG.
  • Each university sets its own minimum marks, subject combination and course rules. See the eligibility on every college card above.

CUET UG 2027 notification date and expected schedule

As of 7 November 2026, NTA has not published the CUET UG 2027 notification. Education portals that track the exam expect the notification in January 2027, registration in the weeks after it, the exam in May 2027 and the result in June 2027. These are estimates built on the 2026 cycle, when the exam ran from 11 May to 7 June and the result came on 23 June 2026.

Check the official NTA CUET website for the final dates.

CUET 2027 syllabus and previous year papers

Domain subject papers follow the NCERT Class 12 syllabus. The language papers and the General Aptitude Test follow the syllabus NTA gives in the Information Bulletin. For previous year papers, look on the official NTA CUET website, then practise with Exampoll mock test PDFs in the same 60-minute format.

CUET 2027 official links: university list, cut-off and syllabus

  • Participating university list: NTA CUET (UG) official website, see the list of universities accepting CUET scores.
  • CUET syllabus: official subject-wise syllabus on the NTA CUET website, listed under the Information Bulletin and syllabus section.
  • CUET cut-off: cut-offs are released by each university, so check the admission page of the college you want, for example University of Delhi admissions.

Also see our CUET colleges list to compare fees and eligibility.

CUET 2027 exam pattern and marking

  • Language: test in the language you choose, such as English or Hindi.
  • Domain-Specific Subjects: subject papers based on your stream and course, such as Accountancy, Economics, History, Political Science, Physics, Chemistry, Maths or Biology.
  • General Aptitude Test: logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, general knowledge and current affairs.
  • Papers and time: each paper has 50 multiple-choice questions and runs for 60 minutes. A candidate can choose up to 5 papers.
  • Marking: +5 for each correct answer and -1 for each incorrect answer, as per the recent pattern.

How to get admission through CUET

  1. Register for CUET UG on the official NTA website and choose your subjects.
  2. Appear in the exam and download your scorecard when it is released.
  3. Shortlist colleges that accept CUET using the filters on this page.
  4. Apply on each participating university's admission portal within its dates.
  5. Complete counselling or seat allotment and pay the fees to confirm your seat.

FAQ

CUET 2027 frequently asked questions

Quick answers about CUET 2027. Need more help?

Is 700 a good score in CUET?

There is no single good score. It depends on your course, the subjects you took and the cut-off of the university you want. A score that is safe for one college can fall short for another, so check last year's cut-off for each college on your list and aim a little above it.

Can I crack CUET in 1 month?

A month is short but useful if your Class 12 basics are in place. Use the first 10 days to revise NCERT chapters for your subjects, the next 15 days for timed mock tests and checking every mistake, and the last 5 days for weak topics. How far your score rises depends on where you start.

When will the CUET UG 2027 notification be released?

NTA has not released the CUET UG 2027 notification yet. Education portals expect it around January 2027, with registration soon after, but this is an estimate from earlier years. Confirm the dates on cuet.nta.nic.in.

How many students appeared in CUET UG 2026?

NTA reported 15,68,867 registered candidates and 11,64,098 candidates who appeared in CUET UG 2026. The result was declared on 23 June 2026.

How many subjects can I choose in CUET?

CUET UG 2026 offered 37 papers: 13 languages, 23 domain subjects and one General Aptitude Test. A candidate could choose up to 5. Check the 2027 Information Bulletin for any change.

Where can I find the CUET syllabus?

Domain subject papers follow the NCERT Class 12 syllabus. The language and General Aptitude Test syllabus is given by NTA in the Information Bulletin on cuet.nta.nic.in.

Where can I get CUET previous year question papers?

Check the official NTA CUET website for the papers it has published. Then practise in the same style with timed subject-wise mock tests on Exampoll.

What is the full form of CUET?

CUET stands for Common University Entrance Test. It is a national entrance test used by participating universities for undergraduate admission.

Which colleges accept CUET scores?

Many central, state, deemed and private universities admit through CUET UG. Use the filters on this page to see colleges marked as accepting CUET, with eligibility, fees and official links.

Who conducts CUET UG?

CUET UG is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). All dates, notices and forms are published on the official NTA CUET website.

What is the CUET exam pattern and marking scheme?

CUET UG is a computer-based test with multiple-choice questions. It has three parts: Language, Domain-Specific Subjects and the General Aptitude Test. As per the recent pattern, each correct answer gives 5 marks and each wrong answer costs 1 mark. Always confirm the latest pattern in the NTA information bulletin.

Who is eligible for CUET 2027?

Students who have passed Class 12 from a recognised board, or are appearing in 2027, can apply. NTA does not set an age limit, but individual universities may set their own minimum marks and rules.

Is there an age limit for CUET?

NTA does not set an age limit for CUET UG. Some universities may have their own age or marks conditions, so check the college page before you apply.

When is the CUET 2027 exam?

The CUET 2027 exam is expected in May 2027, but NTA has not announced the schedule yet. In 2026 the exam ran from 11 May to 7 June. Please check the official NTA website for the final dates.

Can I get admission to private universities through CUET?

Yes. Many private universities accept CUET scores along with central and state universities. Each college sets its own eligibility, fees and process, which you can compare on this page.

Is there free CUET study material?

Can I order CUET books at home?

Can I download CUET mock tests?

Yes. Mock tests are available as PDF downloads so you can print them and practise at home.
